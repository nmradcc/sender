I'm glad there is no rush on this one. In re using 0.1uF caps: 
Current wisdom out this way is to use a random mix of .1 & .01 caps 
for bypass as the mix has a better quieting effect on the board than 
using all one value or the other. 

I assume that since the board is for use by most anyone, the parts 
will all be through hole types. Also, as a cost issue for the 
hobbiest, I assume you will want a simple 2 sided board. I do not 
know if there are any FCC issues with the board application, but if 
there are, you may need to go multi-layer with internal ground and 
power planes. Are FCC and CE ratings required for this board?

One thing I _always_ end up changing on a schematic that has an XT 
connector arrangement is the symbol itself. I have attached a small 
.OLB file with XT connectors for your use. I created the symbols with 
version 7.01 but will work with 7.1 & 7.2 as well. I have 7.01 and 
7.2 loaded on my machine now.

The reason I use two symbols is so that my PCB program doesn't go 
ape. The fingers are created as SMT parts, and the program will not 
allow a single instance of an SMT part to occupy both sides of a 
board at the same time! Picky little sucker...
